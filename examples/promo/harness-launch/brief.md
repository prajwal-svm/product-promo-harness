# Brief: harness-launch

- **What and for whom:** a 10 s launch sting for this harness, for developers and makers on X and YouTube
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps
- **Length:** 10 s (5 bars at 120 BPM)
- **Style pack:** saas-kinetic, with a neo-brutal neon-lime look of its own: paper white, lime #b8ff2c, black borders,
  hard offset shadows, blob floods, kinetic type, a jelly cursor; no blur, no outline rings
- **Language:** en (on-screen text only, no narration)
- **Voice:** none (music-led)
- **Call to action:** the repository and its licence on the end card
- **Must say (with sources):** open source, MIT (`LICENSE`); one script becomes a video with voice, music, sound
  effects and captions at 60 fps (`README.md`)
- **Must not say or show:** competitors; anything the harness does not do
- **Real product UI sources:** replicas of the harness's own concepts (a script file, a timeline, a render bar) and
  stills of a film it made
