# Story

- **Logline:** one script in, a launch video out, and it's open source.
- **Mechanism:** a script card is typed, one click makes the video: the timeline fills (voice, music, SFX,
  captions), the render runs, a finished film plays.
- **Proof:** the player shows real frames of a film made with the harness.
- **Visual concept:** a neon-lime world where every surface floods in like liquid and every action lands on a beat.

## Beats (120 BPM, a bar is 2 s)

| bar | what happens | events |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | the star pops, sparks fly; it blooms into a lime flood; "One script." scrambles in | e-logo, e-flood, e-word |
| 2 | paper floods back, the script card springs up and types itself, the cursor clicks "Make the video" | e-wipe, e-type, e-click |
| 3 | the card opens into a timeline; voice, music, SFX and captions fill on the beat | e-tracks, e-voice ... e-caps |
| 4 | Render is clicked, the progress bar runs, the player opens with the finished film | e-render, e-done |
| 5 | the lime flood again; "Open source.", the line and the repository; the star returns | e-flood2, e-end, e-sub, e-star |
