# saas-kinetic

A kinetic SaaS launch look, measured from real launch videos (cut rhythm, type scale, colour, sound).

## Look
Warm off-white, full-bleed lime and one dark forest beat. White UI cards with soft wide shadows.
Lime pill buttons with dark ink. Lime 3D props, sharp, layered by scale and parallax. Two-tone
sentence-case headlines with one lime key word.

## Voice and text
Register `ad-sparse`, text mode `paraphrase`: headlines restate the VO in fewer words.

## Overrides
- `idle-sine-loop` re-allowed for background props only (see `structure.json`).
- `shockwave-ring` re-allowed for the single thin ring wipe (see `structure.json`).

## Quality checklist
- Every chapter has its headline→chip orientation header.
- The capsule cursor is the only pointer.
- Exactly one accent key word per headline.
- No hard cuts.
- Headline hits land within one frame of their beat.
- The showreel question: would a motion designer put this shot on their reel?
